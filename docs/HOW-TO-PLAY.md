# How to play Thornwild

## Getting started
Pick **New Game** and choose a save slot. A short storybook opens the journey, then you're in
**the Rootwild**. Reach the goal gate at the end of each level; some levels end with a guardian.
From the **world map** you can replay levels, visit **Pim's Stall** (outfits and upgrades),
take on the **★ Daily** challenge, and visit **🏡 Kiri's Grove**.

## Moving like a forest spirit
- **Jump:** hold longer to jump higher.
- **Wall cling:** hold toward a wall in mid-air to cling. Jump to kick off, or keep holding
  toward the wall and jump to climb-kick upward.
- **Sprout Dash:** a burst in any direction (F/E plus a direction, or ➶ on touch). You get one
  in the air, refreshed when you land.
- **Ground pound:** press ↓ in mid-air. It defeats critters below you, sets off Echo Totems,
  wakes Lumen Blooms and **breaks cracked floors**.
- **Roll:** the action button on the ground. Great for barrelling through critters.

Guardians teach new moves: **Thornclaws** (hang on walls and climb freely) and **Comet Dash**
(dash through critters, two air dashes).

## Heroes: swap any time with Q / ⇄
| Hero | Good at |
|---|---|
| **Kiri** | All-rounder. Wisp Leap (a second jump), Rootgrip on glowing moss, the Lumen Song (hold ↑) |
| **Pip** | Hold jump to glide. Action fires an acorn sling; in the air it's a swooping strike. Small enough for **tiny tunnels** |
| **Brom** | Rolls **smash cracked walls**; his ground pound is a **Quake** that hits everything nearby |

Pip joins after the Rootwild guardian, and Brom after the Weeping Ruins guardian.

## Companions: walk into a cage to free one
| Companion | Ability (action button) |
|---|---|
| **Grumbo** the Horned Beast | Charge: smash cracked walls and anything in the way |
| **Boing** the Tree Frog | Huge jumps, wall jumps, tongue-grapple glowing hookblooms |
| **Sola** the Gliding Bird | Flap and glide, ride updrafts (she tires after a while) |
| **Nuu** the River Otter | Fast swimming, torpedo dash through underwater barriers |
| **Oru** the Ancient | Flip gravity and walk on ancient stone ceilings |

Hop off with C or ⏏.

## Combos and supers
Stomping, bouncing and knocking out critters without a pause builds a **combo**:
NICE → GREAT → AWESOME → WILD! → LEGENDARY. When it ends you're paid in glims.
Everything you do fills the **Lumen meter** under your hearts. When it glows, press **V**
(or tap **✸**) for your hero's super:
- ☀ **Kiri:** *Sunburst*, rings of light that clear everything nearby and pull in glims.
- 🌰 **Pip:** *Acorn Storm*, three seconds of acorns raining down.
- ⛰ **Brom:** *Earthquake*, a leap and a crash that splits the ground and breaks cracked stone.

## Things to find
- **Glims:** the glowing drops everywhere. Every 100 gives you a heart.
- **Seed Coins:** three per level. They grow Kiri's Grove and buy Lumen Tree skills.
- **Sun Shards, Echoes and the Star Heart** for the story.
- **Secrets:** hidden rooms, hero vaults under the road, high ledges.
- **Root Hollows:** stand on one and press ↓ to dive into a mini-game.

Lost? Pause and open the **Journal → Quest** page. It lists what's still hidden in the level,
with a direction and distance to each.

## Interactive objects
Walk near something you can use and a card floats above it telling you what it is and which
button to press. The button names match your device.

## The Journal (pause)
Esc / P / ❚❚ opens it. Flip pages with Q / E, the shoulder buttons, or the tabs:
**Quest · Gear · Lumen Tree · Abilities · Team · System** (settings live in System).

## Settings
Master, music and sound-effect volume · music on/off · screen shake (full/low/off) ·
soft flashes · relaxed game speed · big touch buttons · graphics quality (title menu).

## Seed Clash
From the title screen. Up to 4 players (keyboard, gamepads, or CPU) brawl on a jungle stage.
Knock opponents off with attacks, specials and team "bond" moves, and avoid the Bramble King.
