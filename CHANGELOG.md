# What's new in Thornwild

Patch notes, newest first.

## v1.5 · "Connected Worlds" (Oct 10)
- 🎬 **A real title screen:** an animated logo with a light sweep and vine ornament, drifting glowing motes, the forest visible behind, and "press any key / tap to begin" before the menu slides in. Menu buttons highlight with a soft sound.
- 🍂 **Atmosphere:** leaves drift and tumble through the forests (some right past the camera), glowing spores rise in the caves.
- ⚑ **Beacon warp:** stand at any lit beacon and press ↑ to warp to another one you've lit in that level. Beacons you've lit stay lit when you come back.
- 🌿 **Side branches:** six hidden climbs up into the treetops (Rootwild, Canopy, Ruins, Heart of the Seed), each ending in a grove full of glims that counts as a secret.
- ➡️ **Onward:** after clearing a level, go straight into the next one without visiting the map.
- 🔇 **Fixed the cricket noise:** the background hiss is now a soft breeze that only plays during gameplay, follows the sound-effects volume, and all audio stops when you switch away.

## v1.4.4 · Portal back in (Oct 10)
- 🌀 **Pick up where you left off:** leaving a level for the world map remembers exactly where you were, and who you were riding. Press Play and choose **Continue where you left off**, any **beacon** you've lit in that level, or the **start of the level**.
- The world map now opens on the level you just left.

## v1.4.3 · Save fix (Oct 9)
- 💾 **No more lost progress:** the game now saves the moment you leave or close the app, every 15 seconds while you play, and at checkpoints, Sun Shards, secrets and guardian wins. Before, mid-level progress was only saved when you reached the world map or finished a level, so phones that closed the browser in the background could lose it.
- The game asks the browser to keep its save data instead of clearing it when space runs low.

## v1.4.2 · Clear touch controls (Oct 9)
- 📱 **Touch controls you can actually see:** the joystick now has a solid dark backing, bright rim, direction arrows and a MOVE label; Jump, Action and Dash buttons have high-contrast backs and labels. Visible on any background.
- 👆 **First-time coach:** a pulsing "drag to move" bubble sits on the joystick until you use it once.
- Fix: the level timer no longer overlaps the pause button on phones.

## v1.4.1 · Polish (Oct 5)
- 💢 **Hits feel like hits:** a red flash and a tiny freeze-frame when you're hurt; on your last heart the screen edges pulse and a soft heartbeat plays.
- 🎮 **Rumble:** gamepads rumble and phones buzz on big impacts (follows the screen-shake setting).
- ⏸ **Auto-pause:** switching apps or tabs mid-level opens the Journal instead of letting the game run on.
- 📱 **Auto graphics:** if the game stutters on your device, graphics step down automatically (switch it off in Journal › System). Graphics quality can now be changed in the Journal too.
- 🎓 New tips in the Rootwild teach Sprout Dash and the ground pound, with buttons that match your device.

## v1.4 · "Home and Glory" (Oct 5)
- 🏡 **Kiri's Grove:** a home island that grows with every Seed Coin (27 things to grow), where
  friends move in and guardians leave trophies.
- 💥 **Combos and supers:** chain moves from NICE to LEGENDARY for glim payouts. Fill the Lumen
  meter and unleash Sunburst, Acorn Storm or Earthquake.
- ★ **Daily challenge:** a new twist on a level every day, with streak rewards.
- 🎵 **Music for every area**, from Rootwild marimba to Mine clanks to the Heart choir.
- ⚙️ **Settings in the Journal:** separate volumes, screen shake, soft flashes, big touch buttons.
- 🐾 Smoother companions; Pip scurries and Brom lumbers.
- 🔒 **Nine hero vaults:** cracked floors to pound through, Pip-only tunnels, Brom-only digs.
- 👑 **Guardian cinematics:** title-card entrances, enraged phases with unique attacks, and
  slow-motion finishing blows.
- ⬇️ **Ground pound on ↓** in mid-air for every hero.

## v1.3 · "The Journal" (Oct 4)
- A Zelda-style pause Journal: Quest, Gear, Lumen Tree, Abilities, Team, System.
- Floating prompts over interactive objects that show the exact button to press.
- Fix: level tips no longer pop up in the wrong world (e.g. Ruins tips in the Skyward Isles).
- Animation pass: blended poses, run twist, skids, apex float; enemies notice you and turn smoothly.
- Environment passes for every biome and a living world map (boats, gulls, windmill, fish).
- Redesigned heroes, companions, critters and guardians.
- World map touch controls: drag, pinch, flick, edge arrows.

## v1.2 · "Crossroads" (Oct 3)
- ⚔️ **Seed Clash:** a local 4-player brawler with 10 fighters.
- Ori-style wall cling and climb-kicks; **the Thornwell**, a deep Metroid-style descent.
- Unique hero kits: Pip's acorn sling and swoop, Brom's Quake.
- Pim's wardrobe with a 3D try-on preview; Lumen Tree skills and charms.
- Level results cards, goal gates, ghost races, a collectibles finder.
- Six hidden Easter eggs.

## v1.1 · "The World Map" (Sep 28–30)
- An eight-level world map with area guardians, underwater and flight worlds.
- Title menu, save slots, storybook prologue, local co-op.
- Seed Coins, Pim's shop, hats, time medals, photo mode, Root Hollow mini-games.
- Joystick controls, Sprout Dash, Thornclaws and Comet Dash.
- HD rendering: image-based lighting, ambient occlusion, soft shadows.
- Pip and Brom join as swappable heroes; a 3D diorama world map.

## v1.0 · "The Lumen Seed" (Sep 27)
- The original journey: six connected biomes, five companions, Echo Totems, Lumen Blooms,
  the Colossus finale and three endings.
